# Mission Room: What We Do and What We Can Demo

Sep 28, 2026 · @James

## Summary

Prescience Technology sells Immersive Project Controls (IPC): a Mission Room wall (hardware and software from Mission Room) wrapped in Prescience services (process optimisation, data insight, facilitation and training). The one full delivery on record is CPB / ActivUs (Logan and Gold Coast Faster Rail). There, Prescience supplied the room, ran familiarisation sessions on 15 Oct 2025, built a PowerPoint template and a six-part tutorial series, and proposed six scenarios.

What we have shown ourselves, on camera, is narrower than the marketing footage suggests:

- **Filmed in our Hub or at our booth:** pen markup, the radial and side launchers, multi-screen layouts, the three-wall PowerPoint template, troubleshooting, Oracle Aconex model and clash review, and Novade dashboards and defects (Sydney Build 2024).
- **Vendor footage only:** BIM/4D, 360° site and route capture, digital rehearsal, control-room dashboards and training quizzes. All of it is Mission Room UK footage of UK projects (HS2 Euston, TransPennine, Network Rail).
- **Not filmed anywhere:** Oracle P6 or Primavera Cloud schedules running in the room. The launcher has an "OPC & Whiteboard" shortcut, but there is no footage of it in use.

The demo menu below keeps these apart so nobody promises a demo we have not run.

## What we do

The room is a 6 m × 2 m three-wall projected screen (the "Cine" setup), run from one Windows PC, with four infrared pens, infrared cameras and short-throw projectors (Tutorial #1, 00:48–01:46). Anything that runs on Windows can run on the walls. The value we sell is the session around it, not the screen.

**Services around the room**

- **IPC offer:** process optimisation, data analysis and insight, executive facilitation and training by Prescience and Prescience 3PO, with Mission Room supplying hardware and software (IPC 04 video; IPC "What is it" doc, Aug 2024).
- **Pilot then embed:** a structured pilot of up to two weeks, then consulting to develop, embed and train three use cases (Aug 2024 offer).
- **Client activation (CPB / ActivUs):** supply, implementation, familiarisation sessions, cheat sheets and FAQ, tutorial videos, scenario and template design, quarterly "What's New" updates.
- **Prospect demos in our rooms:** about 11 demos logged May 2024 to May 2025, in the Milton (Brisbane) and Homebush (Sydney) rooms.

For pricing or commercial terms, go to the sales team; they are deliberately left out here.
